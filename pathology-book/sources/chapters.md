# Pathology: All-in-One — chapter plan (Special Pathology, 4th Year MBBS)

Syllabus source: the user's 4th-year syllabus (BUMDC pattern; UHS-compatible). Lectures (L), interactives (I) and labs (Lab) per module are listed under each chapter, so every syllabus item lands in exactly one chapter. Standard text: Robbins (Basic Pathology / Pathologic Basis of Disease, 10th ed).

Target length: **8–10 rendered pages** per chapter (chapters marked ★ may reach 11–12). Exemplar: ch16 (12–13 pages because it covers four lectures).

## Part A · Gastrointestinal Tract (Module 10)
- **ch01 Oral Cavity, Salivary Glands & Oesophagus** — L: Introduction to special pathology (1 short box only), Diseases of salivary glands, Diseases of oesophagus · I: Oral pre-malignant lesions; Barrett's oesophagus & GERD. Cover: leukoplakia, erythroplakia, oral SCC (naswar/gutka/paan, betel quid), oral submucous fibrosis; sialadenitis, mumps, sialolithiasis, Sjögren (brief), pleomorphic adenoma, Warthin tumour, mucoepidermoid carcinoma, adenoid cystic; oesophagus: achalasia, hiatus hernia, Mallory–Weiss, varices, oesophagitis (reflux, eosinophilic, infectious), GERD, Barrett, SCC vs adenocarcinoma.
- **ch02 Stomach: Gastritis, Peptic Ulcer & Gastric Tumours** ★ — L: Gastritis/acid peptic disease (×2), Gastric carcinoma, H. pylori gastritis · Lab: H. pylori gastritis. Cover: acute gastritis, stress ulcers (Curling, Cushing), chronic gastritis (H. pylori vs autoimmune), PUD (pathogenesis, morphology, complications), gastric polyps, gastric adenocarcinoma (Lauren intestinal vs diffuse, signet ring, linitis plastica, Krukenberg, Virchow node, Sister Mary Joseph), MALT lymphoma, GIST, carcinoid (NET) brief.
- **ch03 Malabsorption & Inflammatory Bowel Disease** ★ — L: Malabsorption diseases, IBD. Cover: approach to malabsorption, coeliac disease, tropical sprue, lactase deficiency, Whipple disease, abetalipoproteinaemia (brief), Crohn vs ulcerative colitis (table), complications, colitis-associated neoplasia; infective/pseudomembranous colitis (brief, for differential); intestinal TB (Pakistan) as a Crohn mimic.
- **ch04 Intestinal Polyps & Colorectal Carcinoma** — L: Intestinal polyps, Colorectal carcinoma. Cover: non-neoplastic polyps (hyperplastic, hamartomatous: juvenile, Peutz–Jeghers, inflammatory), adenomas (tubular, villous, tubulovillous, sessile serrated), FAP, Gardner, Turcot, Lynch (HNPCC), adenoma–carcinoma sequence (APC → KRAS → TP53), MSI pathway, CRC morphology (right vs left), staging (TNM, Dukes for viva), spread, CEA.

## Part B · Liver, Gallbladder & Pancreas (Module 10)
- **ch05 Jaundice & Viral Hepatitis** ★ — L: Jaundice · I: Viral hepatitis · Lab: Chronic hepatitis. Cover: bilirubin metabolism, pre-hepatic/hepatic/post-hepatic jaundice (table with lab values), hereditary hyperbilirubinaemias (Gilbert, Crigler–Najjar, Dubin–Johnson, Rotor), neonatal jaundice (brief), LFT patterns; hepatitis A–E (table), HBV serology (graph of markers over time), acute vs chronic hepatitis morphology (ballooning, Councilman bodies, ground-glass hepatocytes, interface hepatitis, lymphoid aggregates in HCV), grading/staging, fulminant hepatitis.
- **ch06 Cirrhosis, Haemochromatosis & Hepatocellular Carcinoma** ★ — L: Haemochromatosis, HCC · I: Cirrhosis · Lab: Cirrhosis, HCC. Cover: cirrhosis definition, causes, pathogenesis (stellate cells), micro/macronodular, portal hypertension (flowchart of consequences), liver failure, alcoholic liver disease & NAFLD (brief), haemochromatosis (HFE C282Y, hepcidin, Prussian blue, bronze diabetes), Wilson disease & α1-antitrypsin (short table), HCC (HBV/HCV/aflatoxin, AFP, morphology, fibrolamellar).
- **ch07 Gallbladder & Pancreas** — L: Diseases of gallbladder · I: Chronic pancreatitis · Lab: Chronic pancreatitis. Cover: gallstones (cholesterol vs pigment; risk 4F/6F), acute & chronic cholecystitis, complications, gallbladder carcinoma (high in Pakistan/north India), acute pancreatitis (aetiology GET SMASHED, pathogenesis flow, morphology fat necrosis, labs), chronic pancreatitis (causes, morphology, complications, pseudocyst), pancreatic carcinoma (brief: KRAS, Trousseau, Courvoisier).

## Part C · Lung (Module 10)
- **ch08 Pneumonia & Tuberculosis** ★ — I: Pneumonia · Lab: Pneumonia, Pulmonary TB. Cover: classification of pneumonia (CAP, HAP, aspiration, atypical, immunocompromised), lobar pneumonia stages (congestion, red & grey hepatisation, resolution), bronchopneumonia, complications, lung abscess (brief); TB pathogenesis (primary vs secondary, Ghon complex, granuloma, type IV hypersensitivity), morphology (caseation, Langhans giant cells), progressive primary, miliary, cavitary; diagnosis (ZN, GeneXpert); Pakistan TB burden.
- **ch09 COPD, Asthma & Interstitial Lung Disease** ★ — I: COPD-I · L: Interstitial lung diseases. Cover: obstructive vs restrictive (table + spirometry), emphysema (types, protease–antiprotease, α1-AT), chronic bronchitis (Reid index), asthma (atopic vs non-atopic, Curschmann spirals, Charcot–Leyden crystals), bronchiectasis; ILD: idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (UIP, honeycomb), pneumoconioses (coal, silicosis, asbestosis), sarcoidosis, hypersensitivity pneumonitis, cor pulmonale.
- **ch10 Lung Tumours** — I: Lung tumours · Lab: SCC lung. Cover: classification (NSCLC: adeno, SCC, large cell; SCLC; carcinoid), risk factors (smoking), molecular (EGFR, ALK, KRAS; TP53, RB in SCLC), morphology & sites (central vs peripheral), spread, paraneoplastic syndromes (table), Pancoast, SVC syndrome, Horner; metastatic tumours, mesothelioma (asbestos).

## Part D · Blood & Lymphoid System (Modules 10–11)
- **ch11 Anaemias I: Approach, Iron Deficiency, Megaloblastic & Aplastic** ★ — L: Anaemias-I/II · Lab: Anaemias. Cover: definition, classification (morphological + aetiological), red cell indices, peripheral smear vocabulary, iron metabolism & hepcidin, IDA (stages, labs), anaemia of chronic disease (vs IDA table), megaloblastic (B12 vs folate, pernicious anaemia, hypersegmented neutrophils, neuro), aplastic anaemia, myelophthisic (brief).
- **ch12 Anaemias II: Haemolytic Anaemias & Thalassaemias** ★ — L: Thalassaemias, Anaemias-II. Cover: haemolysis features (intra vs extravascular), hereditary spherocytosis (osmotic fragility), G6PD (Heinz bodies, bite cells, fava/drugs), sickle cell (HbS, crises), thalassaemias (α and β genetics, β-major vs minor, crew-cut skull, HbF/HbA2, Hb electrophoresis, iron overload), AIHA (warm/cold, Coombs), PNH (brief), microangiopathic (link to ch15). Pakistan thalassaemia burden & carrier screening.
- **ch13 Leukaemias & Myeloproliferative Neoplasms** ★ — L: Leukaemia-I/II · Lab: Leukaemias. Cover: classification, ALL (TdT, children, t(12;21), Ph+), AML (≥20% blasts, Auer rods, MPO, t(15;17) APL with DIC, t(8;21)), CLL (smudge cells, CD5+CD23), CML (t(9;22) BCR-ABL, imatinib, phases), MPNs (PV, ET, PMF – JAK2), MDS (brief), leukaemoid reaction vs CML table.
- **ch14 Lymphomas** ★ — L: Lymphoma-I/II. Cover: lymphadenopathy approach, Hodgkin (Reed–Sternberg CD15/CD30, subtypes, staging Ann Arbor, B symptoms), NHL classification (WHO), follicular t(14;18) BCL2, DLBCL, Burkitt t(8;14) MYC starry sky, mantle cell t(11;14) cyclin D1, MALT, CLL/SLL, T-cell (brief), multiple myeloma & plasma cell neoplasms (CRAB, Bence Jones, M spike, punched-out lesions), HL vs NHL table.
- **ch15 Bleeding Disorders** — L: Bleeding disorders. Cover: haemostasis recap, tests (BT, PT, aPTT, platelet count, TT) interpretation table, vascular purpuras (scurvy, HSP), thrombocytopenia (ITP acute vs chronic, TTP/HUS), platelet function (Bernard–Soulier, Glanzmann), coagulation (haemophilia A/B, von Willebrand), vitamin K deficiency, liver disease, DIC (pathogenesis flow, labs).

## Part E · Kidney & Urinary Tract (Module 11)
- **ch16 Glomerular Diseases: Nephritic & Nephrotic Syndromes** ★ — EXEMPLAR (written).
- **ch17 Tubulointerstitial Disease, Cystic Kidney & Renal Function** ★ — L: Tubulointerstitial nephritis I/II · I: Cystic diseases of kidney, ATN, RFTs & acid–base balance. Cover: ATN (ischaemic vs nephrotoxic, phases), acute & chronic pyelonephritis (ascending/haematogenous, VUR, papillary necrosis, coarse scars, thyroidisation), drug-induced interstitial nephritis, analgesic nephropathy; cystic diseases (ADPKD PKD1/PKD2, ARPKD, medullary sponge, simple cysts, acquired dialysis cysts); renal function tests (urea, creatinine, eGFR, urinalysis, casts table), acid–base (ABG approach flowchart, anion gap, compensation formulae), AKI vs CKD.
- **ch18 Renal Tumours, Urothelial Carcinoma & Urolithiasis** — L: RCC, Urolithiasis · I: TCC · Lab: RCC, transitional cell carcinoma. Cover: RCC (clear cell VHL 3p, papillary MET, chromophobe), paraneoplastic, Wilms tumour (WT1, triad), angiomyolipoma/oncocytoma (brief); urothelial carcinoma (papillary vs flat CIS, risk: smoking, aniline dyes, cyclophosphamide, Schistosoma → SCC), grading; urolithiasis (types table: calcium oxalate, struvite, uric acid, cystine; causes; complications; Pakistan "stone belt"), hydronephrosis.

## Part F · Reproductive System & Breast (Module 11)
- **ch19 Prostate & Testis** — L: Diseases of prostate, Testicular tumours · I: Congenital testicular diseases · Lab: Seminoma, BPH. Cover: prostatitis, BPH (DHT, transition zone, nodules), prostate carcinoma (peripheral zone, Gleason, PSA, osteoblastic mets), cryptorchidism, testicular atrophy, orchitis (mumps, TB), torsion, testicular tumours (classification GCT vs sex-cord stromal; seminoma vs NSGCT table; markers AFP, hCG, LDH; ITGCN), hydrocele/varicocele brief.
- **ch20 Vulva, Cervix & Endometrium** ★ — L: Diseases of vulva and cervix, Diseases of endometrium, Endometrial cancer · Lab: Adenomyosis & endometrial carcinoma. Cover: vulva (lichen sclerosus, VIN, SCC brief), cervix (HPV 16/18 E6/E7, CIN/SIL grading, Pap smear, carcinoma cervix, screening, HPV vaccine), endometrium (endometritis, adenomyosis, endometriosis — sites, chocolate cyst, theories), endometrial hyperplasia, endometrial carcinoma type I vs II, leiomyoma vs leiomyosarcoma.
- **ch21 Ovarian Tumours & Gestational Trophoblastic Disease** — L: Ovarian tumours · Lab: Mucinous cystadenoma ovary, trophoblastic diseases. Cover: non-neoplastic cysts, PCOS (brief), classification of ovarian tumours (surface epithelial, germ cell, sex cord-stromal, metastatic), serous vs mucinous, teratoma (mature cystic), dysgerminoma, yolk sac, granulosa cell, fibroma (Meigs), Krukenberg; markers (CA-125); GTD: complete vs partial mole (table), invasive mole, choriocarcinoma (hCG).
- **ch22 Breast** ★ — L: Breast carcinoma · I: Benign epithelial lesions · Lab: Fibroadenoma, breast carcinoma. Cover: inflammatory (acute mastitis, fat necrosis, duct ectasia), fibrocystic change (non-proliferative vs proliferative with/without atypia, risk table), fibroadenoma vs phyllodes, intraductal papilloma, carcinoma (risk factors, BRCA1/2, DCIS vs LCIS, IDC NST, ILC — E-cadherin, special types, molecular subtypes ER/PR/HER2, Ki-67, prognostic factors, TNM), male breast; Pakistan (highest breast cancer incidence in Asia).

## Part G · Endocrine System (Module 11)
- **ch23 Thyroid & Parathyroid** ★ — L: Non-neoplastic lesions of thyroid, Neoplastic diseases of thyroid · I: Diseases of parathyroid glands · Lab: Hashimoto, papillary carcinoma. Cover: thyroid function tests, hyper- vs hypothyroidism, Graves, Hashimoto (Hürthle cells, lymphoid follicles), subacute granulomatous (de Quervain), Riedel, goitre (diffuse/multinodular; iodine deficiency), adenoma vs follicular carcinoma (capsular/vascular invasion), papillary (Orphan Annie nuclei, psammoma, BRAF, RET/PTC), medullary (C cells, calcitonin, amyloid, MEN2), anaplastic; parathyroid: primary/secondary/tertiary hyperparathyroidism ("stones, bones, groans, moans"), hypoparathyroidism.
- **ch24 Pituitary, Adrenal & Diabetes Mellitus** ★ — L: Diabetes mellitus, Diseases of pituitary, Adrenal disorders I/II. Cover: pituitary adenomas (prolactinoma, GH: gigantism/acromegaly, ACTH), hypopituitarism (Sheehan), posterior pituitary (DI, SIADH); adrenal: Cushing (types, flowchart), hyperaldosteronism (Conn), adrenogenital/CAH (21-hydroxylase), Addison (acute: Waterhouse–Friderichsen; chronic), phaeochromocytoma (rule of 10s), MEN syndromes table; DM: classification, type 1 vs type 2 pathogenesis, diagnostic criteria (ADA), complications (AGEs, polyol), diabetic nephropathy/retinopathy/neuropathy, macrovascular.

## Part H · Cardiovascular System (Module 12)
- **ch25 Blood Vessels: Atherosclerosis, Aneurysms, Vasculitis & Vascular Tumours** ★ — L: Atherosclerosis, Aortic aneurysm & dissection, Vasculitis, Vascular tumours. Cover: arteriosclerosis types, atherosclerosis risk factors, pathogenesis (response-to-injury flowchart), plaque morphology, stable vs vulnerable plaque, consequences; hypertensive vascular disease (hyaline vs hyperplastic arteriolosclerosis); aneurysms (AAA, syphilitic, berry), aortic dissection (Marfan, cystic medial degeneration, DeBakey/Stanford); vasculitis classification by vessel size (table: GCA, Takayasu, PAN, Kawasaki, GPA, MPA, EGPA, HSP, Buerger), ANCA; vascular tumours (haemangioma, pyogenic granuloma, Kaposi sarcoma HHV-8, angiosarcoma, glomus).
- **ch26 Heart: Ischaemic, Hypertensive, Valvular Disease & Cardiomyopathies** ★ — L: Ischaemic heart disease · I: Hypertensive & valvular heart diseases, Cardiomyopathies. Cover: IHD spectrum, MI (pathogenesis, timeline of morphology table, enzymes graph, complications by day), hypertensive heart disease (concentric LVH), cor pulmonale; rheumatic fever/RHD (Jones criteria, Aschoff bodies, Anitschkow cells, mitral stenosis), infective endocarditis (acute vs subacute, Duke), calcific aortic stenosis, mitral valve prolapse; cardiomyopathies (dilated, hypertrophic — β-myosin, asymmetric septal; restrictive — amyloid), myocarditis (brief).

## Part I · Musculoskeletal System (Module 12)
- **ch27 Bone: Metabolic Disease, Osteomyelitis & Bone Tumours** ★ — L: Osteoporosis, Bone tumours · I: Rickets, osteomalacia & osteomyelitis · Lab: Osteochondroma, osteosarcoma, giant cell tumour of bone, bone tumours. Cover: osteoporosis (primary/secondary, RANKL/OPG), Paget disease (brief), rickets & osteomalacia (vitamin D, rachitic rosary, Looser zones), osteomyelitis (pyogenic: sequestrum, involucrum, Brodie abscess; TB/Pott); bone tumours by site (illustration of epiphysis/metaphysis/diaphysis), osteoid osteoma, osteochondroma (EXT1/2), enchondroma, osteosarcoma (Codman triangle, sunburst), chondrosarcoma, Ewing (t(11;22), onion-skin), giant cell tumour (soap-bubble, RANKL), metastases.
- **ch28 Joints & Skeletal Muscle** — L: Arthritis, Gout & gouty arthritis · I: Muscular dystrophies & myopathies. Cover: OA vs RA (table), RA pathogenesis & morphology (pannus, rheumatoid nodule, RF/anti-CCP), seronegative spondyloarthropathies (brief), septic & TB arthritis, gout (uric acid, tophi, negatively birefringent needle crystals) vs pseudogout (CPPD, positive rhomboid); muscle: neurogenic vs myopathic atrophy, Duchenne vs Becker (dystrophin, Gower sign, pseudohypertrophy), myotonic dystrophy, inflammatory myopathies (polymyositis, dermatomyositis, inclusion body), myasthenia gravis (brief).

## Part J · Nervous System (Module 12)
- **ch29 CNS I: Cerebrovascular Disease, Infections, Demyelinating & Degenerative Disease** ★ — L: Cerebrovascular diseases, Demyelinating disorders, Neurodegenerative diseases · I: Meningitis & brain abscess. Cover: cerebral oedema, raised ICP & herniation (brief), hypoxia/ischaemia, infarction (thrombotic vs embolic, red vs pale, morphology timeline), haemorrhage (intracerebral hypertensive, SAH berry aneurysm), meningitis (acute pyogenic vs aseptic vs TB — CSF table), brain abscess; MS (plaques, oligoclonal bands), other demyelinating (ADEM brief); Alzheimer (plaques, tangles), Parkinson (Lewy bodies), Huntington, ALS (brief).
- **ch30 CNS II: Brain Tumours & Nerve Sheath Tumours** — L: Brain tumours I/II · Lab: Schwannoma, brain tumours. Cover: general features (no lymphatics, location matters, WHO grades, adults supratentorial vs children infratentorial), gliomas (astrocytoma IDH, GBM pseudopalisading necrosis, pilocytic astrocytoma Rosenthal fibres, oligodendroglioma 1p/19q fried-egg, ependymoma rosettes), medulloblastoma (Homer–Wright, subgroups), meningioma (psammoma, whorls, NF2), CNS lymphoma, metastases; schwannoma (Antoni A/B, Verocay), neurofibroma, NF1 vs NF2.

## Part K · Skin (Module 12)
- **ch31 Skin** ★ — L: Introduction to skin diseases, Melanocytic naevi & malignant melanoma, Inflammatory dermatoses, Bullous disorders, Skin cancers · I: Benign epithelial tumours of the skin · Lab: Malignant melanoma. Cover: terminology (hyperkeratosis, parakeratosis, acanthosis, spongiosis, acantholysis), acute (urticaria, eczema, erythema multiforme) & chronic dermatoses (psoriasis — Munro microabscesses, Auspitz; lichen planus — sawtooth, Civatte bodies, Wickham striae), bullous: pemphigus vulgaris vs bullous pemphigoid vs dermatitis herpetiformis (table, IF), benign epithelial tumours (seborrhoeic keratosis, keratoacanthoma), actinic keratosis, SCC, BCC (palisading), naevi (junctional, compound, intradermal, dysplastic), melanoma (ABCDE, growth phases, Breslow, BRAF).
