  • Open with a definition or classification in one or two lines; the first mark is usually there.
  • Draw one labelled flowchart for pathogenesis and one table for any “compare” or “differentiate”: both are quicker to mark than paragraphs.
  • Morphology always in two halves: gross (size, colour, consistency, cut surface) then microscopy (architecture → cells → special features).
  • Finish with complications or prognosis, and one marker, gene or Pakistan-relevant risk factor where it fits.
