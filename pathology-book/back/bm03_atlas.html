Pathology: All-in-One · Rapid revision

Lab OSPE Atlas

Every slide, smear and specimen of the Special Pathology lab list, described the way you will see it and the way the examiner wants to hear it.

1. Organ (what normal tissue is left at the edge?) → 2. Process (inflammation, hyperplasia or tumour?) → 3. Benign or malignant (circumscribed vs infiltrative; atypia, mitoses) → 4. Name it + two features you can point at → 5. Answer the add-on question (cause, marker, complication). In each card below, LP = low power, HP = high power, ID = the three points to write.

M10 Module 10 · GIT, liver, lung, blood

H. pylori gastritisCH 2 · SLIDE

LPAntral mucosa with a dense band of lymphocytes and plasma cells in the superficial lamina propria; lymphoid follicles with germinal centres.

HPNeutrophils inside gland and pit epithelium; small curved rods in the surface mucus.

ID1 lymphoplasmacytic infiltrate + follicles 2 intraepithelial neutrophils (activity) 3 organisms on Giemsa or silver stain

Examiner asksTwo serious complications? → Peptic ulcer; gastric adenocarcinoma; MALT lymphoma.
Chronic pancreatitisCH 7 · SLIDE

LPLobules replaced by dense fibrous tissue; few atrophic acini; islets spared and crowded together.

HPDilated ducts containing pink protein plugs or calcified concretions; scattered chronic inflammatory cells.

ID1 fibrosis with acinar loss 2 dilated ducts with protein plugs 3 preserved islets trapped in scar

Examiner asksCommonest cause and two complications? → Long-term alcohol; pseudocyst, steatorrhoea, diabetes.
Chronic viral hepatitisCH 5 · SLIDE

LPPortal tracts expanded by lymphocytes (dense lymphoid aggregates in HCV); inflammation spills into the lobule; fibrous septa later.

HPLymphocytes eroding the limiting plate (interface hepatitis); spotty necrosis; ground-glass hepatocytes (HBV) or steatosis (HCV).

ID1 portal lymphocytic infiltrate 2 interface hepatitis 3 portal or bridging fibrosis

Examiner asksGrade vs stage? → Grade = activity (inflammation, necrosis); stage = fibrosis, ending in cirrhosis.
CirrhosisCH 6 · SLIDE

LPNormal lobules lost; whole section made of regenerative nodules of hepatocytes ringed by fibrous septa (trichrome blue).

HPSepta carry lymphocytes and proliferating bile ductules; nodules lack a central vein; fatty change or Mallory–Denk bodies if alcoholic.

ID1 diffuse process 2 bridging fibrous septa 3 parenchymal nodules without normal architecture

Examiner asksThree effects of portal hypertension? → Ascites, varices (oesophageal, rectal, caput medusae), splenomegaly, encephalopathy.
Hepatocellular carcinomaCH 6 · SLIDE

LPThick trabeculae and sheets of liver-like cells separated by sinusoids; cirrhotic liver alongside.

HPPolygonal cells, granular pink cytoplasm, high N:C ratio, prominent nucleoli; bile plugs; vascular invasion.

ID1 trabeculae more than 3 cells thick, reticulin lost 2 atypical hepatocytes 3 bile production

Examiner asksMarker and local risk factors? → AFP; HCV (leading in Pakistan) and HBV cirrhosis, aflatoxin.
Lobar pneumoniaCH 8 · SLIDE

LPEvery alveolus in the field filled with exudate; alveolar walls intact, so the outline of the lung is preserved.

HPRed hepatisation: neutrophils, red cells, fibrin. Grey hepatisation: fibrin mesh with dying neutrophils, red cells lysed.

ID1 diffuse alveolar exudate 2 septa intact 3 stage-specific contents

Examiner asksName the stages and the usual organism. → Congestion, red and grey hepatisation, resolution; Streptococcus pneumoniae.
Pulmonary tuberculosisCH 8 · SLIDE

LPMany rounded granulomas, some fusing, with pink centres; fibrosis around older lesions.

HPAmorphous pink caseation; rim of epithelioid histiocytes, Langhans giant cells (horseshoe nuclei), lymphocytes.

ID1 caseous necrosis 2 epithelioid cells + Langhans cells 3 lymphocyte cuff and fibrosis

Examiner asksStain and mechanism? → Ziehl–Neelsen for AFB; type IV hypersensitivity (Th1, IFN-γ activates macrophages).
Squamous cell carcinoma, lungCH 10 · SLIDE

LPNests and sheets of malignant squamous cells infiltrating a desmoplastic stroma near a bronchus.

HPKeratin pearls, single-cell keratinisation, intercellular bridges; pleomorphic nuclei, mitoses.

ID1 invasive squamous nests 2 keratin pearls 3 intercellular bridges

Examiner asksTypical paraneoplastic syndrome? → Hypercalcaemia from PTHrP; strongest risk factor smoking.

M11 Module 11 · Kidney, reproductive tract, breast, thyroid

Renal cell carcinoma, clear cellCH 18 · SLIDE

LPNests and sheets of cells separated by a delicate branching network of thin-walled vessels; haemorrhage; pseudocapsule.

HPCells with water-clear cytoplasm (glycogen, lipid), crisp cell membranes, small round nuclei.

ID1 clear cells with sharp borders 2 rich delicate vasculature 3 haemorrhage, necrosis

Examiner asksGene and paraneoplastic effects? → VHL (3p); polycythaemia (EPO), hypercalcaemia (PTHrP).
Urothelial (transitional cell) carcinomaCH 18 · SLIDE

LPPapillary fronds with fibrovascular cores covered by thickened urothelium; stromal invasion in high-grade tumours.

HPLoss of polarity, nuclear pleomorphism, hyperchromasia and mitoses at all levels.

ID1 papillae with fibrovascular cores 2 thick disordered urothelium 3 atypia ± invasion

Examiner asksRisk factors? → Smoking, aniline dyes, cyclophosphamide, phenacetin; Schistosoma → squamous carcinoma.
SeminomaCH 19 · SLIDE

LPSheets and lobules of uniform cells divided by thin fibrous septa full of lymphocytes; granulomas may be present.

HPLarge round cells, clear glycogen-rich cytoplasm, distinct borders, central nucleus with a prominent nucleolus.

ID1 uniform clear cells 2 lymphocyte-rich septa 3 granulomatous reaction

Examiner asksMarkers and prognosis? → AFP normal; hCG mildly up in some; PLAP, OCT3/4+; radiosensitive, excellent outlook.
Benign prostatic hyperplasiaCH 19 · SLIDE

LPNodules of glands and fibromuscular stroma; glands of varying size, some cystic with papillary infoldings.

HPTwo cell layers: inner tall columnar secretory cells and outer flat basal cells; corpora amylacea in lumens.

ID1 nodular gland + stroma hyperplasia 2 basal cell layer preserved 3 infoldings, corpora amylacea

Examiner asksHormone and zone? → Dihydrotestosterone; transition zone, so it obstructs the urethra early. Not premalignant.
AdenomyosisCH 20 · SLIDE

LPIslands of endometrium deep in the myometrium, well below the endometrial–myometrial junction, with thick muscle around them.

HPBenign endometrial glands surrounded by endometrial stroma; no atypia.

ID1 glands and stroma 2 within myometrium 3 reactive muscle hypertrophy

Examiner asksDifference from endometriosis? → Adenomyosis is inside the uterine wall; endometriosis is outside the uterus (ovary, pouch of Douglas).
Endometrial carcinoma (endometrioid)CH 20 · SLIDE

LPCrowded back-to-back glands with little or no stroma, cribriform areas, invading myometrium.

HPStratified atypical columnar cells, mitoses; squamous morules may be present.

ID1 back-to-back glands 2 cytological atypia 3 myometrial invasion

Examiner asksType I vs type II? → I: oestrogen, hyperplasia, PTEN, good prognosis. II: serous, atrophic endometrium, TP53, aggressive.
Mucinous cystadenoma, ovaryCH 21 · SLIDE

LPMultiple cyst spaces with fibrous walls, lined by a single layer of epithelium.

HPTall columnar cells full of apical mucin with small basal nuclei; no stratification, atypia or invasion.

ID1 multilocular cysts 2 single layer of tall mucinous cells 3 bland basal nuclei

Examiner asksHow does it differ from serous? → Mucinous: larger, multilocular, rarely bilateral, mucin. Serous: often bilateral, ciliated cells, psammoma bodies.
Complete hydatidiform moleCH 21 · SLIDE

LPLarge swollen chorionic villi with central fluid-filled cisterns; no fetal vessels.

HPCircumferential proliferation of cytotrophoblast and syncytiotrophoblast around villi, with atypia.

ID1 hydropic villi with cisterns 2 diffuse trophoblast proliferation 3 no fetal parts

Examiner asksKaryotype and follow-up? → 46,XX all paternal; serial hCG, as some progress to invasive mole or choriocarcinoma.
ChoriocarcinomaCH 21 · SLIDE

LPHaemorrhagic, necrotic tumour; no chorionic villi.

HPSheets of malignant cytotrophoblast mixed with multinucleate syncytiotrophoblast, invading vessels.

ID1 absent villi 2 biphasic trophoblast 3 extensive haemorrhage and necrosis

Examiner asksMarker and spread? → Very high hCG; early blood spread to lungs, vagina, brain; very chemosensitive.
Fibroadenoma, breastCH 22 · SLIDE

LPWell-circumscribed nodule; loose stroma compresses ducts into slits (intracanalicular) or surrounds round ducts (pericanalicular).

HPDucts with epithelial and myoepithelial layers; bland stroma without atypia or mitoses.

ID1 circumscribed biphasic lesion 2 slit-like ducts, two cell layers 3 bland stroma

Examiner asksDifferentiate from phyllodes tumour? → Phyllodes: older women, leaf-like clefts, cellular atypical stroma, stromal overgrowth, can recur.
Invasive ductal carcinoma (NST)CH 22 · SLIDE

LPIrregular cords, nests and glands of tumour cells invading fat in a dense desmoplastic stroma; DCIS nearby.

HPPleomorphic hyperchromatic nuclei, nucleoli, mitoses; no myoepithelial layer; lymphovascular invasion.

ID1 infiltrative edge 2 desmoplasia 3 malignant nests without myoepithelium

Examiner asksPrognostic factors? → Node status (most important), size, grade, LVI, ER/PR, HER2, Ki-67.
Hashimoto thyroiditisCH 23 · SLIDE

LPThyroid replaced by dense lymphoplasmacytic infiltrate with lymphoid follicles and germinal centres; small atrophic follicles.

HPHürthle (oncocytic) cells with abundant granular pink cytoplasm lining follicles; scant colloid.

ID1 lymphoid follicles 2 Hürthle cells 3 follicular atrophy, little colloid

Examiner asksAntibodies and tumour risk? → Anti-TPO, anti-thyroglobulin; B-cell (marginal zone) lymphoma risk raised.
Papillary carcinoma, thyroidCH 23 · SLIDE

LPBranching papillae with fibrovascular cores; scattered laminated psammoma bodies.

HPClear “Orphan Annie-eye” nuclei, overlapping, with grooves and intranuclear pseudoinclusions.

ID1 papillae with cores 2 diagnostic nuclear features 3 psammoma bodies

Examiner asksSpread and risk factor? → Lymphatics to cervical nodes; childhood radiation; BRAF, RET/PTC.
Leiomyoma, uterusCH 20 · SLIDE

LPWell-demarcated nodule of interlacing smooth-muscle bundles in whorls.

HPUniform spindle cells with cigar-shaped nuclei, pink cytoplasm, very few mitoses, no necrosis.

ID1 circumscribed 2 whorled fascicles 3 bland cigar-shaped nuclei

Examiner asksFeatures of leiomyosarcoma? → Tumour necrosis, nuclear atypia, high mitotic count.

M12 Module 12 · Bone, skin, nervous system, heart

OsteochondromaCH 27 · SLIDE

LPCap of hyaline cartilage covered by perichondrium over a stalk of trabecular bone with marrow.

HPCap chondrocytes like a disorganised growth plate; enchondral ossification at the base of the cap.

ID1 cartilage cap + perichondrium 2 enchondral ossification 3 cortex and marrow continuous with host bone

Examiner asksSite and genetics? → Metaphysis near growth plate (knee); multiple hereditary exostoses: EXT1/EXT2.
OsteosarcomaCH 27 · SLIDE

LPDestructive sheet of malignant cells laying down lace-like osteoid; host trabeculae destroyed.

HPBizarre pleomorphic cells, atypical mitoses, tumour giant cells; osteoid made directly by tumour cells.

ID1 malignant osteoid from tumour cells 2 marked pleomorphism 3 bone destruction

Examiner asksAge, site and X-ray? → Under 20, knee metaphysis; Codman triangle, sunburst; lung metastases.
Giant cell tumour of boneCH 27 · SLIDE

LPSheets of mononuclear cells with evenly scattered osteoclast-like giant cells; haemorrhage.

HPGiant cells with up to 100 nuclei that look like the nuclei of the mononuclear cells.

ID1 many uniformly spread giant cells 2 mononuclear stromal cells 3 epiphyseal site (clinical)

Examiner asksDriving molecule? → RANKL from neoplastic stromal cells; age 20–40, soap-bubble X-ray, recurs locally.
Ewing sarcomaCH 27 · SLIDE

LPSheets of small round blue cells divided by fibrous strands; necrosis.

HPUniform cells a little larger than lymphocytes, scant clear glycogen-rich cytoplasm; Homer Wright rosettes.

ID1 small round blue cells 2 PAS+ glycogen, CD99+ 3 Homer Wright rosettes

Examiner asksTranslocation and X-ray? → t(11;22) EWSR1–FLI1; diaphysis, onion-skin periosteum.
Malignant melanomaCH 31 · SLIDE

LPAsymmetric lesion; atypical melanocytes in nests and singly at all levels of the epidermis (pagetoid) and invading dermis; no maturation with depth.

HPLarge cells, pleomorphic nuclei, cherry-red nucleoli, mitoses, variable melanin.

ID1 pagetoid spread 2 no maturation 3 atypia, mitoses, melanin; S-100, HMB-45+

Examiner asksSingle best prognostic factor? → Breslow thickness; also ulceration, mitotic rate, nodes.
SchwannomaCH 30 · SLIDE

LPEncapsulated spindle-cell tumour with cellular Antoni A and loose myxoid Antoni B areas; thick hyalinised vessels.

HPPalisaded nuclei in two rows with pink fibrillary zone between them: Verocay bodies.

ID1 capsule 2 Antoni A + B 3 Verocay bodies; S-100 diffusely +

Examiner asksCommonest intracranial site? → Vestibular nerve at cerebellopontine angle; bilateral = NF2.
GlioblastomaCH 30 · SLIDE

LPVery cellular infiltrating glioma with necrosis; tumour cells line up around necrotic zones.

HPPleomorphic astrocytic cells, mitoses, glomeruloid microvascular proliferation.

ID1 pseudopalisading necrosis 2 microvascular proliferation 3 pleomorphic GFAP+ cells

Examiner asksGrade and outlook? → WHO grade 4; butterfly lesion; median survival about 15 months.
MeningiomaCH 30 · SLIDE

LPLobules and concentric whorls of meningothelial cells; laminated psammoma bodies.

HPSyncytial cells with indistinct borders, oval nuclei, occasional pseudoinclusions.

ID1 whorls 2 psammoma bodies 3 bland syncytial cells

Examiner asksOrigin and genetics? → Arachnoid cap cells; NF2 / chromosome 22 loss; prior radiation.
Pilocytic astrocytomaCH 30 · SLIDE

LPBiphasic: compact areas of bipolar hair-like cells and loose microcystic areas.

HPBright pink corkscrew Rosenthal fibres and eosinophilic granular bodies; few mitoses.

ID1 biphasic pattern 2 Rosenthal fibres 3 granular bodies, bland cells

Examiner asksAge, site, behaviour? → Child’s cerebellum, cyst with mural nodule; BRAF; grade 1, good outlook.
MedulloblastomaCH 30 · SLIDE

LPDensely cellular sheets of small round blue cells in the cerebellar midline.

HPHyperchromatic carrot-shaped nuclei, scant cytoplasm, many mitoses; Homer Wright rosettes.

ID1 small round blue cells 2 Homer Wright rosettes 3 high mitotic rate

Examiner asksHow does it spread? → Through CSF (drop metastases); radiosensitive; WNT group best.
