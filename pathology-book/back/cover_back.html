Pathology: All-in-One · Modules 10–12
Special Pathology, made clear and made to stick.
For students

Fourth year asks you to master the pathology of every organ system in three short modules. This book does the sorting for you. Its chapters follow your syllabus lecture by lecture, explain the why before the what, and end with exactly what you will face in the exam hall.

For teachers

Definitions, classifications and morphology follow the Robbins standard, so what students revise matches what you teach and what examiners mark. Flowcharts, comparison tables and gross-and-microscopy panels make it a ready companion for lectures, interactive sessions and laboratory work.

  • 31 chapters mapped to every lecture, interactive session and lab of Modules 10, 11 and 12
  • Concept Clear boxes that explain the mechanism behind each disease
  • Pathogenesis flowcharts, mind maps and “A vs B” comparison tables
  • Gross and microscopy panels in the words examiners reward
  • Lab Spotter boxes for naming slides and specimens in seconds
  • An Exam Zone in every chapter: solved SEQs, BCQs with reasons, viva questions and OSPE stations
  • A Night-before page and spaced-revision tracker for every chapter
  • Exam craft guide, syllabus map and ready-made study plans
