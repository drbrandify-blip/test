Reference standard and independence

Definitions, classifications and morphological descriptions in this book follow the standard set by Robbins Basic Pathology and Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Disease (10th editions), the standard pathology texts in Pakistani medical colleges. Students are encouraged to read Robbins alongside this book. Pathology: All-in-One is an independent review written in its own words, with original diagrams, tables and questions. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Elsevier or by the authors or editors of Robbins; the name is used only to identify the reference text. Examination questions in this book are original practice questions written in the style of university papers; they are not reproductions of any past paper.
