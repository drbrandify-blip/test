Pathology: All-in-One · Before you begin

Syllabus map

Every lecture, interactive session and lab of the Special Pathology syllabus, matched to the chapter that teaches it.

Find the week you are in, read across to the chapter number, and study that chapter alongside the lecture. Chapters follow organ systems, so a chapter may collect topics taught in different weeks (for example, Chapter 24 gathers the pituitary and diabetes lectures of Module 11 and the adrenal lectures of Module 12).

Robbins standardDefinitions, classifications, morphology and figures in every chapter follow Robbins Basic Pathology and Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Disease (10th editions), the texts your teachers and examiners use; each chapter opener names the matching Robbins chapter.
